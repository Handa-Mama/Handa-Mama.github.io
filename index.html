About & privacy

හඳ මාමා කතන්දර is a free collection of Sinhala bedtime stories. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Settings and the last page read are kept only on this device. Once a book has been opened, it can be read again without the internet.

Tip: swipe, drag a page corner or use the arrow buttons to turn pages.
